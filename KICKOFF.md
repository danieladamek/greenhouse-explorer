## 1. Hard rules

- **Never invent scientific content.** Everything comes from `content-pack/`. A null field or a `TODO(author)` renders amber (`.bx-todo`) and lists on `/methods`; never fill it from your own knowledge. `physchem` is null on all 49 compounds; many evidence lists are empty by design with a `gaps` entry saying so; 34 of the 47 taxa have no profile at all.
- **Body text is rendered as written.** `review.md`, compound prose, preparation prose, safety blocks: structure, link, render — never rewrite.
- **Uncited prose fails the build** (APP-SPEC §6.1): any block of ≥ 25 words in `review.md` with no `[n]` and no `<!-- framing -->` marker is a content-build error. The pack ships at 0 validator errors; do not "fix" a citation gap by adding a citation.
- **`<!-- synthesis -->` blocks stay visible** (there are 4), each with an id, all linked from `/methods`.
- **Nothing implies peer review or UAH endorsement.** `manifest.venue` says it in capitals; the banner (§4e) is on every route; `as_of` (2026-09-23) on `/`, `/read`, `/about` and every profile.
- **The content line is content.** Preparation methods appear as extraction chemistry. **No dose, no indication, no "take", no serving, no frequency appears anywhere in the app**, on any surface, in any label. `preparations.yaml` has no such keys (its schema is closed; an unknown key is a build error). Monograph posology appears only inside grade-T evidence rows, quoted as the monograph's. If you find yourself writing a UI label like "how much to use", stop.
- **Evidence never loses its grade or its test article.** A health statement is rendered with its grade badge and test article together, or not at all. A D or E row is never rendered under a heading that implies people ("benefits", "effects"); the heading is "Evidence" and the row says its population.
- **Amounts never lose their basis.** An occurrence value renders with unit **and** basis. `basis: unstated` renders with a "basis not stated in source" label; `presence_only` renders as "reported present" with no number. `/compare` refuses to place rows of different bases in one chart and excludes `unstated` and `presence_only` rows from any quantitative view, saying so.
- **No runtime fetches.** No PubChem, no GBIF, nothing at runtime; everything ships in `dist/`. Structure images come from the build (§4b).
- **Fail loudly in the content build.** Schema violation, unresolved `[n]`, unknown term/concept/compound/taxon id, missing data file, ambiguous term variant, a forbidden key in `preparations.yaml`, a planting whose `taxon_id` is not in `taxa.yaml` → non-zero exit with a clear list. Write `content-pack/BUILD-ERRORS.md` and continue with whatever validated.
- **Delivery mode is `pages`** (§6b). GitHub identity `danieladamek`; `gh auth status` must show it or you stop.
- Vault contract: touch nothing outside this folder except K5's `state`; `notes/`, `source/` and the 🚀 hub note are excluded from the repo via `.git/info/exclude`.

## 4b. Compound catalogue — normative spec (extends psychedelics §4b)

`content-pack/compounds.yaml` is a list of 49 records. Keys: `id`, `name`, `synonyms`, `class`, `subclass`, `one_liner`, `palette_group` (lamiaceae | asteraceae | shared), `auxiliary`, `identity {iupac, formula, mw, mw_source, smiles, inchikey, cas, pubchem_cid, chebi_id, source, cross_checked}`, `physchem {logp, pka, source}` (all null → amber), `biosynthesis {pathway, note}`, `occurrences[]`, `pharmacology {primary_targets, mechanism, functional_notes}`, `absorption_bioavailability`, `evidence[]`, `processing_stability {heat, water_solubility, ethanol_solubility, volatility, enzyme_dependence, formed_during, note, refs}`, `safety_flags[] {flag, detail, threshold_or_limit, source_citation, refs}`, `teaching_note`, `citations[]`, `gaps[]`, `as_of`.

- `occurrences[]` rows: `taxon_id` (must exist in `taxa.yaml`), `plant_part`, `value` (number, range string, or null), `unit`, **`basis` ∈ {fresh_weight, dry_weight, essential_oil_pct, per_g_extract, unstated, presence_only}**, `level` ∈ {species, cultivar}, `cultivar`, `source_citation`, `source_doi_or_url`, `database`, `verified`, `note`. Render every row with unit, basis, level and source. `unstated` and `presence_only` are labelled as such and excluded from quantitative comparison. Never average rows; never convert bases; a range renders as a range.
- `evidence[]` rows: `claim`, **`grade` ∈ {A,B,C,D,E,T}**, **`test_article` ∈ {whole_herb, named_extract, essential_oil, isolated_compound, multi_herb_formula}**, `test_article_detail`, `population` ∈ {human, animal, in_vitro, traditional}, `outcome_summary`, `refs[]`, `note`. Render as a table under the heading **Evidence**, grade badge + test article on every row, population shown; the grade legend (from `scope.yaml.evidence_grades`) one click away and on `/methods`. Null results are results: render `outcome_summary` as written (several B rows are null findings).
- `safety_flags[]` render prominently, with `threshold_or_limit` shown verbatim where present (these are regulatory or toxicological limits, never serving guidance; label them "regulatory / toxicological limit").
- `processing_stability` feeds the "Processing and preparation" section on the compound page and the links from `/tea`; `formed_during: distillation` (chamazulene) gets its own callout.
- Sections on `/compounds/:id`, in order: Structure (3D viewer + 2D, feature C2–C7) · Identity with provenance line · Where it's found (occurrence table; each taxon links to its plant page) · Biosynthesis (links into `fig-pathways`) · Pharmacology (mechanism prose, linked terms) · Absorption and bioavailability · Evidence · Processing and preparation · Safety · Teaching note · What this record does not know (`gaps`, amber) · Sources. No legal-status or clinical-status section exists in this app.
- `/compounds` — filterable grid/table of the 48 non-auxiliary records: structure thumbnail, name, class icon, family colour, plants that contain it (count), `one_liner`; filters: class, family, plant, "has human evidence", "has safety flag"; **filter state in the URL** (feature A2).
- `/compare` — **two directions**, both reachable from the UI and both URL-addressable:
  - `?ids=a,b,c` — 2–4 compounds side by side: structures with synchronised 3D rotation (feature A4), occurrence tables aligned by taxon, evidence rows aligned by grade, safety flags. A quantitative chart appears only when the selected rows share one `basis`; the basis selector is explicit; a mixed selection shows the refusal message from the psychedelics `Compare.tsx`, adapted to bases.
  - `?plants=x,y` — 2–4 taxa side by side: their curated compounds as a presence/amount matrix (basis-aware, same rule), shared vs distinct compounds, their safety blocks, their evidence at grade B or above.
  - Compare tray (feature E2) collects compounds from any page and taxa from any plant page.
